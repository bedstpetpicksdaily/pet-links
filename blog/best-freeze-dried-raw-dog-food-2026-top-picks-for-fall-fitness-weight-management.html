Why Freeze-Dried Raw Works for Weight Management

Weight gain in dogs is rarely about eating too much food — it's usually about eating the wrong kind of food. Many mainstream kibbles rely on corn, wheat, rice and potato starch to hold the shape of the biscuit, which means a significant portion of every meal is fast-digesting carbohydrate. That leads to blood sugar spikes, energy crashes and stored fat. Freeze-dried raw flips that ratio: protein and healthy fats come first, carbohydrates come last or not at all. Dogs feel fuller on smaller portions, maintain lean muscle mass, and burn body fat more efficiently — exactly what you want heading into the low-activity months.

How to Transition and Feed for Fall

Switch gradually over 7–10 days, replacing roughly 25% of the old food every two to three days. Freeze-dried raw can be served dry as a crunchy meal or rehydrated with warm water — rehydrating is our preference in autumn, as the added moisture supports hydration, digestion and a greater feeling of fullness. Weigh your dog every two weeks and aim for a visible waist when viewed from above and easily felt (but not visible) ribs. Pair the diet with consistent daily walks, indoor scent games and short training sessions to keep calories burning even when the weather turns.

What to Look for in a Freeze-Dried Raw Brand

Check that a named meat is the first ingredient, that the brand names its protein sources and origins, and that the recipe meets AAFCO or equivalent complete-and-balanced standards. Avoid vague terms like "meat meal" or "animal by-product," and be wary of added sugars, artificial colours and long chemical preservative lists. Transparency, single-source proteins and grain-free formulation are the hallmarks of a quality product — and they're the standards Zeal Pet Food consistently meets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is freeze-dried raw dog food better than kibble for weight loss?

Generally yes. Freeze-dried raw is higher in protein and lower in starchy carbohydrates than most kibble, which helps dogs preserve lean muscle while burning fat. Dogs also tend to feel fuller on smaller, nutrient-dense portions.

Should I rehydrate freeze-dried raw food?

You can serve it either way. Rehydrating with warm water adds moisture that aids digestion and hydration, increases satiety and boosts aroma for fussy eaters — a great option during cooler autumn months.
