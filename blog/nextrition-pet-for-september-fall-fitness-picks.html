Frequently Asked Questions

What is cold-pressed dog food and why is it better?

Cold-pressed dog food is manufactured at low temperatures (around 90°F), preserving heat-sensitive nutrients, enzymes, and vitamins that traditional kibble processing destroys. This results in more bioavailable nutrition that dogs digest more efficiently, leading to better energy, coat health, and overall wellness.
