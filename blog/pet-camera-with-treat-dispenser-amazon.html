What to Look For

1. Treat Capacity and Compatibility

Not all pet cameras handle treats equally. Look for models with:

  • Adequate reservoir size (250+ treats minimum) so you're not refilling constantly
  • Compatibility with multiple treat sizes and shapes
  • No jamming issues—this is the #1 complaint on reviews
  • Easy-loading design that doesn't require disassembly

2. Video Quality and Night Vision

You need to actually see your pet to know when to dispense treats. Prioritize:

  • 1080p minimum resolution (4K is ideal if budget allows)
  • Wide-angle lens (at least 110-160 degrees) to see more of the room
  • Infrared night vision for 24-hour monitoring
  • Clear audio so you can hear your pet's reactions

3. App Control and Connectivity

The whole point is remote interaction, so the app experience matters tremendously:

  • Intuitive app with one-tap treat dispensing
  • Stable connection on both WiFi 2.4GHz and 5GHz
  • No significant lag between tapping and dispensing
  • Works reliably outside your home network

4. Build Quality and Safety

This device will be around your pet constantly, so safety is non-negotiable:

  • Durable, non-toxic materials that can handle pet curiosity
  • Secure mounting options (shouldn't tip over easily)
  • No sharp edges or small detachable parts
