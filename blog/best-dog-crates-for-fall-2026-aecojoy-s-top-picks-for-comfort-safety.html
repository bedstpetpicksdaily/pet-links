Frequently Asked Questions

What size AECOJOY dog crate should I choose for my dog?

AECOJOY offers crates in multiple sizes from small (for breeds under 25 lbs) to extra-large (for dogs over 100 lbs). Your dog should be able to stand, turn around, and lie down comfortably. Check AECOJOY's sizing guide on their website to match your dog's breed and weight for the perfect fit.

Are AECOJOY crates suitable for outdoor use?

Yes! AECOJOY offers both indoor and outdoor pet furniture. Their outdoor enclosures and runs feature weather-resistant materials, UV protection, and secure construction designed for year-round use. However, always provide shelter and monitor your dog during extreme weather.

How do I introduce my dog to an AECOJOY crate?

Start with positive associations by leaving the door open and placing treats or toys inside. Let your dog explore at their own pace. Gradually increase time inside with the door closed, always using a calm, positive tone. Never force your dog into the crate—patience and positive reinforcement are key to successful crate training.

Can AECOJOY crates be used for travel?

AECOJOY offers portable crate options specifically designed for travel. These lightweight models are easy to transport in vehicles while maintaining the safety and comfort standards the brand is known for, making them ideal for fall road trips and holiday travel.
