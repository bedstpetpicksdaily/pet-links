Why Cold-Pressed Dog Food Matters for Fall Wellness

Cold-pressed dog food differs fundamentally from traditional kibble. Rather than exposing ingredients to high heat during processing, cold-pressing uses hydraulic pressure to gently create nutrient-dense meals. This method preserves heat-sensitive vitamins, enzymes, and beneficial probiotics that often get destroyed during conventional manufacturing. For dogs transitioning into fall, this means better nutrient absorption and improved digestive health.

Nextrition Pet's commitment to gut-healthy dog food goes beyond cold-pressing. Their harvest-inspired recipes include pre- and probiotics specifically formulated to support digestive balance during seasonal changes. When temperatures drop and dogs spend more time indoors, maintaining optimal gut health becomes crucial for immune function and overall vitality.
