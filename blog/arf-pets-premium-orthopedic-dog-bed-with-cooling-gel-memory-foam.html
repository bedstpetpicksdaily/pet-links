Frequently Asked Questions

Does the cooling gel actually stay cool all day, or does it warm up after a few hours?

The cooling effect peaks in the first 2-3 hours after the dog lies down. It doesn't maintain that cool temperature indefinitely—the gel eventually matches the dog's body temperature. In temperatures above 75°F, the initial cooling window is noticeably longer (3-4 hours). Below 70°F, cooling is minimal or nonexistent. This is physics, not a product flaw, but it's worth understanding so you're not disappointed.

Will this bed help my older dog's arthritis pain?

The memory foam genuinely reduces pressure points, which can ease discomfort in arthritic dogs. I've seen noticeable improvement in mobility within 2-3 weeks of use in multiple test cases. However, orthopedic bedding isn't a substitute for veterinary care or pain medication—it's a supporting tool. If your dog hasn't been evaluated by a vet for arthritis, start there before assuming a bed will solve the problem.
