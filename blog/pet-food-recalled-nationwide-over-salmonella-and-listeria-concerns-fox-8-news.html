Pet Food Recall Alert: What You Need to Know Right Now

A nationwide recall of pet food due to Salmonella and Listeria contamination has pet owners rightfully concerned about what they're feeding their beloved companions. If you've recently purchased pet food, it's time to check your pantry and understand what steps to take to keep your pets safe.

Why This Matters

Salmonella and Listeria are serious bacterial pathogens that can affect both pets and humans. Contaminated pet food doesn't just pose a risk to your dog or cat—it can also spread harmful bacteria to your family through cross-contamination. Pet owners need reliable information about which products are affected and how to choose safer alternatives going forward.

Check the product listing's recall section and verify the batch or lot numbers on your current pet food against the FDA's official recall notice before purchasing, as some products from the same brand may still be available for sale despite the recall.

Editor's Tip

What Pet Owners Should Know

  • Check the FDA's official recall list immediately to see if your pet's current food is included
  • Symptoms of Salmonella or Listeria in pets include vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, and loss of appetite—contact your vet if you notice these signs
  • Don't throw recalled food away; contact the manufacturer for proper disposal and potential refunds
  • When switching to new food, do so gradually over 7-10 days to avoid digestive upset
  • Purchase pet food from reputable sources and check expiration dates before buying

Our Product Recommendations

If you need to find a replacement pet food, consider these trusted options available on Amazon:

  • High-quality dry dog food — Search "AAFCO certified dry dog food" to find brands with rigorous safety testing and quality control standards
  • Prescription pet food alternatives — If your pet has sensitivities, search "limited ingredient dog food" for options that reduce contamination risk through simpler formulations
  • Wet/canned pet food from established brands — Search "premium canned dog food" for products from manufacturers with strong safety records and transparent sourcing

Bottom Line

Pet food safety should never be taken for granted. Check recall lists immediately, choose products from manufacturers with transparent safety practices, and don't hesitate to contact your veterinarian with questions about the safest options for your pet.
