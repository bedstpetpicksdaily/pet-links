Is Zeal Pet Food suitable for all dog breeds?

Yes, Zeal Pet Food offers formulations designed for various dog breeds and sizes. Their grain-free and raw options provide balanced nutrition for small, medium, and large dogs. It's recommended to consult with your veterinarian when transitioning to a new food.

How do I transition my pet to Zeal Pet Food?

Gradually introduce Zeal Pet Food by mixing increasing amounts with your pet's current food over 7-10 days. This slow transition helps prevent digestive upset and allows your pet's system to adjust to the new, high-quality ingredients.

What's the difference between raw and freeze-dried Zeal Pet Food?

Raw food maintains moisture content and must be refrigerated, while freeze-dried options remove moisture for extended shelf life and easier storage. Both retain nutritional integrity and provide premium, natural nutrition without artificial processing.
