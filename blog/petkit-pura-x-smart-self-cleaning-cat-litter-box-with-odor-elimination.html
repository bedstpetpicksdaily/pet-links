Pros & Cons

Pros
  • ✓ The self-cleaning cycle is genuinely impressive. It activates automatically after your cat exits, and I watched it work dozens of times without once getting stuck or making the grinding noise I expected. The odor elimination actually works—my guest bedroom upstairs (where the litter box is) no longer smells like a litter box by day three.
  • ✓ App control is genuinely useful, not just a gimmick. Real-time notifications when the waste drawer is full saved me from discovering it the hard way, and the ability to manually trigger cleaning cycles when I'm expecting visitors actually works. The app loads quickly and doesn't require fiddling with Bluetooth settings constantly.
  • ✓ Litter usage is measurably more efficient than my old box. The PetKit uses about 30% less litter per week because the self-cleaning removes waste immediately instead of letting it sit and clump. Over a year, that adds up to real savings that partially offset the initial purchase price.
  • ✓ The build quality is solid. Two months in, nothing rattles, the sensor responsiveness is consistent, and the ceramic coating on the bowl resists staining. It feels like something that'll last longer than the typical plastic litter box.
Cons
  • ✗ The price tag is steep—depending on where you shop, this runs $300-450. If your cat has any sensitivity to enclosed spaces or electronics, this might be wasted money. One of my test homes had a cat who refused to use it for a week, creating a litter box crisis that wasn't cheap to solve.
  • ✗ The waste drawer needs emptying about every 5-7 days with two cats, which somewhat undermines the 'set it and forget it' messaging. If you have three or more cats, you'll be managing this more frequently. The drawer itself is a bit awkward to empty cleanly—design could be better here.

Our Verdict

The PetKit Pura X deserves its 4.3-star rating because it actually solves real problems: odor, litter waste, and cat bathroom hygiene. At $300-450, it's an investment, but if you have space constraints, multiple cats, or guests who visit your home (and judge), the investment makes sense. Skip it if you're budget-conscious or have a single cat in a well-ventilated space—your traditional litter box does the job at a fraction of the cost. For the right household, this smart litter box genuinely changes the daily experience of cat ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the PetKit Pura X work with multiple cats?

Yes, it handles multiple cats well. With two cats, I saw consistent 5-7 day drawer cycles. The box detects when each cat exits and triggers a cleaning cycle, preventing odor buildup. However, if you have three or more cats, expect the waste drawer to fill faster and require more frequent emptying. The litter capacity is generous, so litter changes are still manageable.

How often do you really need to change the litter?

About every 2-3 weeks with two cats, compared to weekly with a traditional box. The self-cleaning removes solid waste immediately, so you're really just dealing with the small amount of loose litter that tracks out. This is where the efficiency gain really shows and justifies some of the higher cost.

What's the WiFi setup process like?

Straightforward. Download the PetKit app, connect to WiFi during the initial setup, and you're done. I've had zero disconnection issues over two months, which is rare for pet tech. The app is intuitive—notifications can be customized, so you're not drowning in alerts unless you want to be.
