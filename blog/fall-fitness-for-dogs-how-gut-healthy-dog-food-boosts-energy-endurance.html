Understanding Gut Health & Dog Performance

Your dog's gut microbiome acts like an internal engine for performance and vitality. A healthy digestive system ensures that essential nutrients—proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals—are properly absorbed and utilized by the body. This is especially crucial during fall when many dog owners increase outdoor activities and exercise routines. Poor digestion leads to nutrient deficiencies, lethargy, and reduced endurance, making even moderate walks feel exhausting for your pup.

Cold-pressed dog food maintains natural enzymes and nutrients that aid digestion better than traditional kibble manufacturing methods.
