Gnarly Pet Jerky vs. Rawhide Chews: Which High-Protein Dog Treat Wins? Gnarly Pet

By Best Pet Picks Daily • September 02, 2026 • Contains affiliate links

Dog owners face a real dilemma when choosing high-protein chews: Gnarly Pet's natural jerky, traditional rawhide chews, or alternatives like bully sticks. Each option promises protein and long-lasting chewing satisfaction, but they differ significantly in ingredients, safety, price, and nutritional value.

We've analyzed Gnarly Pet Jerky against two major competitors—classic rawhide chews and premium bully sticks—to help you make an informed decision based on your dog's age, chewing intensity, and dietary needs. This isn't about declaring one winner; it's about matching the right treat to your dog's specific situation.

Gnarly Pet Jerky: Natural Protein with Minimal Processing

Gnarly Pet Jerky stands out for its commitment to natural ingredients and high protein content, typically ranging from 25-35% protein per serving depending on the variety. The jerky is slow-dried without artificial preservatives, colors, or flavors, making it appealing to owners seeking whole-food options. A 5-ounce package costs approximately $12-15 and provides extended chewing time. The texture is firm but chewable, suitable for adult dogs with good jaw strength. Gnarly Pet sources grass-fed beef and other premium proteins, which justifies the mid-to-premium price point.

Traditional Rawhide Chews: The Budget-Friendly Classic

Rawhide chews remain popular due to affordability—a standard 5-inch chew costs $2-4 per piece—making them accessible for budget-conscious owners. They're highly palatable and dissolve gradually, reducing choking risk compared to older rawhide formulations. However, rawhide is heavily processed (often bleached) and typically contains 15-20% protein, significantly lower than jerky alternatives. Quality varies dramatically by brand; cheaper options may contain chemical residues, while premium rawhide (like Nylabone or West Paw) is safer but prices approach jerky levels. Rawhide also triggers digestive concerns in some dogs and carries a slightly higher risk of intestinal blockages if dogs swallow large chunks.

Bully Sticks: Single-Ingredient Premium Alternative

Bully sticks (made from bull pizzle) offer 20-25% protein in a completely natural, single-ingredient format. Premium brands like Sancho & Lola's or Purina's Beggin' Strips cost $8-18 per stick and are highly digestible. Unlike rawhide, bully sticks are 100% beef with zero additives or processing chemicals. They're excellent for aggressive chewers and suitable for most dogs, including seniors with softer gums (as they soften during chewing). The main drawbacks are their distinctly pungent smell and variable pricing—premium bully sticks can exceed jerky costs while offering less total chewing time due to smaller portions.

Our Verdict

The winner depends on your dog and priorities: Choose Gnarly Pet Jerky if your dog is an adult with strong jaws, you prioritize natural ingredients and higher protein content, and you want versatile treat options beyond simple chewing. It outperforms rawhide on nutrition and ingredient quality. Choose budget rawhide if cost is the primary concern and your dog tolerates processed treats without digestive issues—the trade-off in protein and safety is real but acceptable for many owners. Choose bully sticks if you want a single-ingredient, highly digestible option and don't mind the smell; they're ideal for dogs with sensitivities or those who need gentler chewing experiences.

For most quality-conscious owners with adult dogs, Gnarly Pet Jerky edges out traditional rawhide due to superior nutrition and ingredient transparency—but it's not automatically the best choice for puppies, senior dogs, or owners on tight budgets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gnarly Pet Jerky safe for puppies?

Gnarly Pet Jerky is best suited for adult dogs (1+ year) with developed jaw strength. The firm texture can be choking hazard for puppies. Consult your vet before introducing jerky to dogs under 12 months.

Why is rawhide chew more affordable than Gnarly Pet Jerky?

Rawhide is a processed byproduct requiring less premium sourcing and labor than whole-muscle jerky. Bulk production and lower ingredient costs drive rawhide prices down, though quality varies significantly by brand.

Which treat has the best protein-to-cost ratio?

Bully sticks offer the best single-ingredient value for protein quality, while budget rawhide wins on raw cost per treat. Gnarly Pet Jerky balances both, offering premium nutrition at mid-tier pricing—better value than rawhide per gram of protein.

Can Gnarly Pet Jerky cause digestive issues?

Gnarly Pet's natural, minimally processed formula is generally easier to digest than heavily treated rawhide. However, introduce any new treat gradually and monitor for sensitivities. Individual dogs may react differently based on existing GI health.

How long do these treats last compared to each other?

Gnarly Pet Jerky provides 30-60 minutes of chewing per serving for average dogs. Bully sticks last 20-45 minutes. Rawhide varies (15-90 minutes) by size and dog aggression. Jerky tends to outlast individual rawhide pieces but less than large rawhide chips.
