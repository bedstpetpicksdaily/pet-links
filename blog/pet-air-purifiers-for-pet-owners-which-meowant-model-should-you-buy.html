Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace the HEPA filter in my Meowant air purifier?

Most Meowant HEPA filters last 6-12 months depending on usage and pet density. The brand recommends checking filter condition monthly and replacing when it appears discolored. Washable pre-filters can extend overall filter life.

Can Meowant air purifiers eliminate all pet odors?

Meowant purifiers significantly reduce pet odors when used alongside good pet hygiene practices. For best results with litter box odors, pair your air purifier with Meowant's self-cleaning litter box to address odors at the source.

Are Meowant air purifiers safe for pets?

Yes, Meowant air purifiers are designed with pets in mind. They operate at safe noise levels, use non-toxic materials, and their filtration doesn't release harmful particles. They're completely pet-safe when used as directed.

Which Meowant air purifier model is best for allergies?

Meowant's HEPA-equipped models are ideal for allergy sufferers. Look for units with high CADR ratings for allergens and consider models with medical-grade filtration if you have severe pet allergies.

How do I know which size Meowant air purifier I need?

Check the model's recommended room size in square feet and compare it to your space. For large homes or multiple rooms, consider purchasing multiple units or a larger model with a higher CADR rating.
