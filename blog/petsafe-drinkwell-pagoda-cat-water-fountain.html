Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I actually need to clean this thing?

The ceramic bowl needs a quick rinse every 2–3 days, and the pump cartridge should be rinsed weekly under running water. Once monthly, soak the pump in white vinegar for 30 minutes to prevent mineral buildup. It's more work than a basic bowl, but less complicated than the multi-filter systems on premium models. Budget 10–15 minutes per week.

Will my cat actually use it, or will it just sit there looking pretty?

About 70% of cats show increased water intake with fountains (based on veterinary studies and owner feedback). The Pagoda's cascading design is particularly effective because most cats are attracted to moving water. That said, some cats remain stubbornly bowl-loyal. If your cat has never shown interest in flowing water, test with a friend's fountain first before committing.

Is this better than the cheaper $20 plastic fountains on Amazon?

Yes, if durability matters to you. Plastic fountains start smelling funky around month four, even with regular cleaning, because tiny scratches harbor bacteria. The Pagoda's glazed ceramic resists this. However, if you're a budget-first shopper and plan to replace it annually, the cheaper option might make financial sense. The Pagoda is the better play for anyone keeping a fountain longer than six months.

Does it really need to run 24/7, or can I turn it off at night?

You can turn it off to save electricity (uses about 3 watts), but there's minimal benefit. Most owners leave it running because cats drink most during night hours when everything's quiet. The noise level is low enough that it won't disrupt sleep in most homes. Running it 24/7 costs roughly $3–5 per year in electricity.

How does this compare to the Drinkwell 360 or other Drinkwell models?

The Pagoda is mid-tier in the Drinkwell lineup. The 360 has a larger capacity (170 oz) and triple-filtration, making it better for multiple cats or all-day absence, but it's bulkier and costs $30–40 more. The original Drinkwell Ceramic is nearly identical to the Pagoda but slightly smaller. Choose the Pagoda if space is tight and you have 1–2 cats; pick the 360 if you need all-day coverage.
