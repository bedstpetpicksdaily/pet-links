Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app control actually work, or is it a gimmick?

The app control works reliably enough for practical use. You can trigger throws from inside, which is the main appeal—useful for rainy days or when you're cooking. That said, the WiFi connection needs to be reasonably strong. If your router is more than 30-40 feet away from the launch area, you might experience drops. The remote backup control is actually the smarter choice if your WiFi is flaky.

How durable is this thing if my dog jumps on it or chews the remote?

The launcher unit itself is plastic but sturdy enough to handle normal use. The real durability concern is the remote—it's not chew-proof, so if your dog is a teether, don't leave it lying around. The launcher mechanism can handle hundreds of throws, though some owners report the ball feeder wearing down after 6+ months of heavy daily use. Replacement parts exist but cost extra.

Will this wear my dog out enough to skip our morning walk?

No. Automatic ball launching is exercise, but it's not a walk replacement. Dogs need the mental stimulation, sniffing, and environmental variety that walks provide. Use this as a supplement on days you're slammed with work, not as your entire exercise routine. A 15-20 minute session might tire out a retriever partially, but most dogs still need additional activity or they'll drive you crazy by evening.
