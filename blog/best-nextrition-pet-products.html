Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Nextrition Pet's cold-pressed dog food different?

Cold-pressing is a manufacturing process that uses low temperatures to preserve nutrients, enzymes, and vitamins that are often lost in high-heat kibble production. Nextrition Pet's cold-pressed formulas maintain ingredient integrity, which may improve digestibility and nutrient bioavailability for your dog.

Is Nextrition Pet good for dogs with sensitive stomachs?

Yes, Nextrition Pet specifically formulates their products with gut health in mind. Their natural ingredients and cold-pressed processing make their food easier to digest, making it a solid choice for dogs with digestive sensitivities or food allergies.

What supplements does Nextrition Pet offer?

Nextrition Pet offers a range of pet supplements designed to complement their dog food, including options for joint support, immune health, and digestive wellness. These supplements use natural ingredients and are formulated to work synergistically with their cold-pressed diet.

How does Nextrition Pet's pricing compare to other premium dog food brands?

Nextrition Pet is positioned at the premium end of the market. While their cold-pressed formulas and natural ingredients justify the higher price, budget-conscious owners may want to compare cost-per-serving with other natural or grain-free brands.

Can I transition my dog to Nextrition Pet food quickly?

As with any dog food change, a gradual transition over 7-10 days is recommended to avoid digestive upset. Start by mixing a small amount of Nextrition Pet food with your dog's current food, slowly increasing the ratio until the transition is complete.
