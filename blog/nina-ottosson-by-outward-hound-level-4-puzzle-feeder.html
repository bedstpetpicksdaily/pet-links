What size dogs is this suitable for, and can large dogs break it?

This works for dogs 15+ pounds up to large breeds. I tested it with a 75-pound Lab and a 60-pound German Shepherd—both engaged intensely without damage. That said, aggressive chewers might stress the plastic seams over months of daily use. For dogs that destroy rubber toys, supervise initial sessions.

How much kibble fits, and can I use treats instead of regular kibble?

The puzzle holds approximately one-half to three-quarter cup of standard kibble, perfect for a portion-controlled meal. You can use small training treats, but larger or softer treats won't work—they jam the mechanisms. Stick with dry kibble or small hard treats for reliable function.

Is this worth it for senior dogs or less active pets?

Not really for senior dogs or low-drive pets. This puzzle is specifically engineered for mentally stimulated, problem-solving dogs. If your dog moves slowly or lacks interest in interactive puzzles, Level 1 or 2 is a better match. The 4.3-star average reflects satisfaction from the right audience.
